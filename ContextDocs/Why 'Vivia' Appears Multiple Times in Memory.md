### What this teaches you about save block management

Stepping back to draw the bigger picture, what you're almost certainly looking at is Pokémon Platinum's save block architecture as it appears in working RAM. The game's save data is organized into "blocks" — discrete chunks of related information like trainer info, party Pokémon, PC boxes, items, and so on. At runtime, the game keeps these blocks in RAM and works on them in place. Several factors lead to multiple copies coexisting.

First, the save system maintains primary and backup copies for corruption recovery — this is the dual-save-block scheme I mentioned.
